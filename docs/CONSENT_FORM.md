# Commit user study — participant consent form

**Study:** Detecting working conditions (neutral / interruptions / time pressure) from computer activity counts.
**Researchers:** the Commit student team.

## What you will do
About 3.5 hours: 15 minutes of normal light work, three 45-minute writing blocks (one without a deadline,
one with chat/e-mail messages to answer, one with a visible countdown) in a random order, a 30-minute video
block, and a short questionnaire at the end. After each block you rate your mental effort.

## What is recorded
Per minute: **counts** of key presses, corrections, shortcuts, pauses, clicks, scrolls, mouse distance, the
**name** of the app in front, and app switches. Website **domains and categories** (no titles, no full
addresses). Your effort ratings and questionnaire answers.

**Never recorded:** what you type, passwords, screenshots, camera, microphone, page titles, URLs.

## How your data is used and kept
- Stored under an ID (P1–P5, D1–D2), never your name.
- Only the per-minute counts file (no titles) leaves the study laptop, for analysis by the research team.
- Used only for this college project. Deleted when the project is graded.

## Your rights
Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your data to be
deleted at any time before it is anonymised in the final report.

## Not an evaluation of you
The study measures the software, not you. Results are never used to judge your performance.
